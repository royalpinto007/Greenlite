Why Greenlite

A human in the loop, without the desk.

Your agents move fast. Greenlite makes sure the calls that need a person actually reach one, with everything needed to decide on the spot.

Real-time escalations

Anything an agent can't safely auto-handle shows up instantly, with full context attached.

Approve, edit or deny

One tap to greenlight the proposed action, tweak it first, or turn it down with a reason.

Ask AI first

Not sure? Ask the built-in assistant for a recommendation before you commit to a decision.

Approval alerts

Keep risky actions visible in the queue and route urgent decisions to the right operator surface.

Two surfaces, one approval layer

The desktop console is a full operator control tower: review queues, audit logs, policy editor, and multi-agent visibility. The mobile app is the fast approve-on-the-go cockpit, native on iOS and Android from the same Expo codebase.
